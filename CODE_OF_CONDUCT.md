# 行为准则 / Code of Conduct

[中文](#中文) | [English](#english)

## 中文

本准则改写自 [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)（[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)）。

### 我们的承诺

我们希望每个人都能无骚扰地参与 Covey 的社区，不论年龄、体型、可见或不可见的残障、民族、性征、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育背景、社会经济地位、国籍、外貌、种族、宗教、性取向或性别身份。我们用开放、友好、多元、包容、健康的方式行事和交流。

### 我们鼓励的行为

- 对他人表示体谅和尊重。
- 给出和接受有建设性的反馈，并大方接受别人的指正。
- 为自己的错误负责，向受影响的人道歉，并从中学习。
- 关注对整个社区最好的结果，而不只是对个人最好的结果。

### 不可接受的行为

- 带有性意味的语言或图像，以及任何形式的性关注或性骚扰。
- 捣乱、侮辱或贬低性的评论，人身攻击或政治攻击。
- 公开或私下的骚扰。
- 未经明确许可，公开他人的私人信息，如住址、邮箱、账号、聊天内容。
- 其他在专业场合中会被合理认为不妥的行为。

### 执行范围与责任

项目维护者负责阐明和执行这些标准，并对他们认为不当、有威胁、冒犯或有害的行为采取恰当、公平的纠正措施，包括编辑或删除不符合本准则的评论、提交、代码、Issue 和其他内容，并在合适时说明原因。

本准则适用于本仓库的所有空间（代码、Issue、PR、讨论），也适用于个人在公共场合代表本项目的时候。

### 举报

遇到违反本准则的行为，请用 GitHub 的私密报告告诉维护者：<https://github.com/ericxk/covey/security/advisories/new>（打开后选择报告一个问题，并在描述里写明这是行为准则问题）。所有举报都会被及时、公正地查看，并对举报人保密。

### 后果

维护者按下面四级处理他们认定违反本准则的行为：

1. **纠正**：私下说明问题，并要求公开道歉。
2. **警告**：说明某次或一系列违规的后果，在约定时间内不得与相关人员互动。
3. **临时封禁**：在约定时间内不得参与社区，包括不得与相关人员往来。
4. **永久封禁**：持续违规、骚扰他人或攻击特定群体的，永久不得参与社区。

## English

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

### Our pledge

We want everyone to take part in the Covey community free of harassment, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We act and interact in ways that are open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy.

### Behavior we encourage

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologizing to those affected, and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the whole community, not only for us as individuals.

### Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a home address, email address, account or chat content, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

### Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers clarify and enforce these standards and take appropriate, fair corrective action in response to behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. That includes removing, editing or rejecting comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code of conduct, and explaining why when appropriate.

### Scope

This code applies in every space of this repository (code, issues, pull requests, discussions), and when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

### Reporting

Report violations to the maintainers privately through GitHub's private reporting: <https://github.com/ericxk/covey/security/advisories/new> (start a report and say in the description that it is a code of conduct matter). All reports are reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy is respected.

### Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers follow these four steps for conduct they find in violation:

1. **Correction**: a private note explaining the violation, with a request for a public apology.
2. **Warning**: a warning with consequences for continued behavior; no interaction with the people involved for an agreed period.
3. **Temporary ban**: a temporary ban from the community for an agreed period, including no interaction with the people involved.
4. **Permanent ban**: a permanent ban for a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward classes of individuals.
